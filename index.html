Simple by design

One report. The right people. Faster action.

The interface is intentionally simple so someone with limited digital experience can report an issue without navigating a complicated form.

1

Speak or type

Choose your language and describe the issue using voice or text.

2

Understand

Speech is converted to text, then translated into a standard language for processing.

3

Route

The case is categorised and matched to a relevant department or support organisation.

4

Track

Authorities and NGOs can review, update status and follow the case to closure.
